# Authority and Autonomy

Use `classifyMutation` and `decideAutonomy` before applying governed changes.

## Source authorship

Qali authors and maintains governed project sources by default. Humans are optional editors and may provide authority through recorded decisions. Writing a source is not the same as confirming its claims: provenance and evidence status remain mandatory, and observation alone cannot redefine expected behavior.

## Auto-merge

Evidence-preserving, reversible changes may auto-merge: append observations or evidence, render derived reports, and formatting-only edits.

## Human approval

Require a recorded decision before confirming knowledge, changing expected behavior, updating visual baselines, deleting or skipping tests, filing external tickets, or sending client proposals.

## Prohibited

Never weaken an assertion to obtain a green run. Never hide or relabel a failure, fabricate approval, or promote observed/inferred knowledge without authority.

Blocking open items stop execution work. Discovery and reporting may continue. An override is valid only when `decisions.md` explicitly records `Allow proceed with open questions: yes`.
